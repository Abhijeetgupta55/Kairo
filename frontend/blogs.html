Why Your Website Needs to be Accessible

In frontend development, we often focus on the visual 'wow' factor. But what if a user can't see your site? Or can't use a mouse? Web accessibility (a11y) means building our sites so *everyone* can use them. This includes using proper HTML, making sites keyboard-navigable, and ensuring color contrast. It's not just a 'nice-to-have', it's a core part of being a good developer.
