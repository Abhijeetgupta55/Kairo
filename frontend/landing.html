About Kairo.

At Kairo, we make browsing calm, not chaotic.

KAIRO is built on a simple idea: the internet should work for you, not against you. Instead of juggling dozens of open tabs, KAIRO lets you create personalized collections, manage favorites, and keep your recent pages within reach. You can even attach notes to your groups, making it easy to track ideas, research, or projects.
